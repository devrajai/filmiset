About FilmiSet — data sources, terms, privacy
What this is

FilmiSet is a free way to answer one question: kya dekhein? Pick a mood, a language or a platform and see what is worth your evening — films, web series and Gujarati, Marathi and Bengali natak, all in one list. Nothing is hosted here and nothing is streamed here; every title links out to the service that has it.

Data sources and credits

This is a free, non-commercial project. Nothing on this page is sold, and no title on it is paid for. Title, year, genre and rating data is compiled from IMDb datasets, TMDB, Wikidata (CC0) and TVmaze. Posters and streaming availability are provided by TMDB.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. FilmiSet is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by IMDb, TMDB, Wikidata, TVmaze, or any streaming service named on this page. All titles, names, images and marks belong to their respective owners and are shown here for identification and reference only.

What this site does not do

We do not host, stream, store or distribute any film, series or audio. There is no player on this page and no download link. Every watch link points to a service that holds the rights (or to an official uploader on YouTube), and watching happens there, under their terms.

Accuracy

Data is compiled automatically and may contain mistakes. Availability, ratings and platform listings change constantly and may be out of date. Nothing here is a warranty of any kind. If something is wrong, use the Report wrong info link on any title and we will correct it.

Terms and privacy

Read the full terms of use and privacy policy.

Corrections and removal

If you own rights to something listed here and want a correction or a removal, write to us through the report link and it will be handled promptly. We would rather remove an entry than keep a wrong one.

How we pick

No title on this page is paid for, and ranking is never for sale. Nothing is promoted because a platform wants it promoted. A pick rises for one reason only: how well it fits what you told us you want right now — your mood, your language, your time, and what you would rather avoid. When we do not know something, we say so instead of guessing, and when a filter has to be loosened to find anything at all, we tell you it was loosened.

Privacy

Your watchlist, star ratings and filters are stored only in your own browser. There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics scripts on this page.
