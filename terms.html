What FilmiSet is

FilmiSet is a free, non-commercial catalogue that helps you decide what to watch. It lists titles, a few facts about each one, and where they may be available. It is a signpost, not a service. Nothing is sold here and no title on it is paid for.

What FilmiSet does not do

  • We do not host, stream, store, upload or distribute any film, series, audio or video file.
  • There is no player on this site and no download link.
  • We do not provide or link to unlicensed sources. Every watch link points to a service that holds the rights, or to an official uploader on YouTube.
  • We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by IMDb, TMDB, Wikidata, TVmaze, or any streaming platform named on this site.

Accuracy — read this one

The catalogue is compiled automatically from public datasets, and it is not perfect. Years can be wrong, a title can be misspelled, and availability changes constantly — a film can leave a platform the day after we list it. Treat the platform shown as a hint, always confirm on the service itself, and use the Report wrong info link on any title when something is off.

Nothing here is a warranty of any kind. We are not liable for a wasted evening, a wrong platform, or a film you did not enjoy.

Content and ratings

Age ratings, content notes and mood tags are our own judgement based on published information. They are guidance, not certification, and they cannot describe every scene. If you are choosing something to watch with children, check the rating on the platform itself.

Health and wellbeing

Some lists on this site are about breakups, grief and mental health. A film can be a comfort, but a film is not care and this site is not therapy. Where those subjects come up we show the national helplines. In an emergency, call 112.

Intellectual property and removal

Titles, names, images and marks belong to their respective owners and are shown here for identification and reference only. If you own rights to something listed here and want a correction or a removal, use the report link on the title and it will be handled promptly. We would rather remove an entry than keep a wrong one.

Changes

These terms may change as the site does. The date at the top is the version you are reading.
