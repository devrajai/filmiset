Privacy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version

There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics scripts and no advertising on this site. What you save stays in your own browser. We do not know who you are and we have no way to find out.

What is stored, and where

Four things are kept in your browser's own storage, on your device:

  • Your watchlist — the titles you saved.
  • Your star ratings — the marks you gave.
  • Your diary — how a film left you feeling, and your "seen" marks.
  • Your last filters — so the site opens the way you left it.

None of this is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes it permanently. There is no server copy to ask us to delete, because there is no server copy.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number or address — we never ask.
  • No account, no login, no password.
  • No advertising identifiers and no ad network.
  • No analytics, no heatmaps, no session recording.

Requests the site makes

The page loads its own files from GitHub Pages, and fonts from Google Fonts. When you open a poster, your browser fetches an image from the host it sits on. Those hosts can see a request coming from your IP address, as any website request does — that is how the web works, and it is not something we add on top.

If you tap a "search this service" link, you leave FilmiSet and that platform's own privacy policy applies from that moment.

Children

The site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone, because we do not collect anything at all.

Contact

For a correction, a removal, or a privacy question, use the Report wrong info link on any title. It reaches the same place.
